## `> about_me`
 
<img src="https://capsule-render.vercel.app/api?type=rect&height=2&color=0:00F0FF,100:7B2FFF" width="100%" alt=""/>
I'm a Computer Engineering student at **Marwadi University** (B.Tech, graduating 2028), based in Gujarat, India. I build full-stack and creative web applications, and I've been shipping them as an independent MERN developer since 2024.
 
- **Full-stack builds:** community marketplaces, e-commerce, and FinTech dashboards, plus an enterprise-style academic portal in ASP.NET Core.
- **Creative web:** Three.js, particle systems, and MediaPipe hand tracking for visually rich interfaces.
- **Applied AI and health-tech:** multilingual voice AI (English, Hindi, Gujarati) and a public-healthcare platform in progress.
- **Cloud:** MongoDB Atlas for data, with deployments on Vercel, Netlify, and Render.
